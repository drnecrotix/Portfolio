# Portfolio v1.1.51

## Homepage screen-by-screen scrolling

This update fixes the homepage section visibility and scroll behavior introduced with the Blog and Projects homepage sections.

### Screen layout
- Hero now occupies a full viewport, so Blog content is not visible before the first downward scroll.
- Blog occupies its own full viewport when its content fits on one screen.
- Projects occupies its own full viewport when its content fits on one screen.
- Longer Blog or Projects content can still extend naturally beyond one viewport instead of being clipped.

### Scroll behavior
- Downward scroll from Hero smoothly moves to Blog, or directly to Projects when Blog is hidden.
- Downward scroll from the end of Blog smoothly moves to Projects.
- Upward scroll from Blog returns to Hero.
- Upward scroll from Projects returns to Blog, or Hero when Blog is hidden.
- Automatic section scrolling is locked briefly during each transition to avoid accidental multi-section jumps from trackpads or repeated wheel events.

### Section headers
- Reduces the small configurable Blog and Projects heading from `text-sm` to a compact `text-[11px]` style.
- Keeps the Blog and Projects content blocks themselves at their existing compact list sizing.

### Upgrade notes
- No Prisma migration is required.
- Homepage visibility and item-count settings are unchanged.
- Version bumped to `1.1.51` for Admin Dashboard update detection.
