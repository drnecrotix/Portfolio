# v1.1.87 - Dedicated Wiki FAQ

## Dedicated FAQ module

- Adds `/wiki/faq` as a first-class part of Necrotix Wiki instead of treating FAQ only as a generic Wiki article category.
- Adds `/admin/wiki/faq` with a compact list-first manager for creating and editing questions.
- Keeps older FAQ-type Wiki articles compatible, while directing new FAQ content to the dedicated manager.
- Reserves the public FAQ route so generic Wiki articles cannot shadow `/wiki/faq`.

## FAQ editing workflow

- Questions are managed from a compact searchable list with one active editor instead of a long landing-settings form.
- Supports rich-text answers through the existing Wiki/Tiptap editor.
- Supports custom categories, keywords, visible/hidden state, featured state, reorder and deletion.
- Page settings, introduction and SEO are collapsed by default.
- Save uses the existing server-action/AJAX pattern and does not reload the page.
- Success and error notifications use an inline toast with save timestamp.
- Adds FormDraftGuard local recovery, unsaved-change warnings and draft commit clearing after a successful save.

## Starter content

The default FAQ includes editable starter questions about:

- Nikola Stoyanov
- Dr Necrotix
- Necrotix Lab
- BG-GAMER
- Kreatrics Technology
- the open-source Portfolio
- Necrotix Wiki and its update model

Starter questions are defaults only. Once saved, the FAQ becomes normal CMS content and every question can be changed, reordered, hidden or removed.

## Public FAQ experience

- Search across questions, answers, categories and keywords.
- Optional category filters.
- Featured questions can be shown first.
- Optional default-expanded answers.
- Responsive accordion layout for desktop and mobile.
- FAQ is linked from the main Wiki biography, article index and individual Wiki articles.

## SEO and structured data

- `/wiki/faq` has canonical metadata, index/follow controls, OpenGraph and Twitter metadata.
- Emits dedicated `FAQPage` JSON-LD from enabled questions.
- Adds the FAQ route to the sitemap only when both the Wiki and FAQ are enabled and FAQ indexing is allowed.
- Individual Wiki articles continue to use their existing Article structured data.
