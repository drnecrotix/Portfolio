# v1.3.15

CMS-aware website configuration, editable service pricing and a broader Lab Services hub.

## Changes

- Adds Forem as a recommended community hub platform alongside WordPress and Discourse.
- Removes the duplicate suggested-functions action and moves recommendations into the functions step.
- Filters functions and add-ons according to the selected CMS.
- Reduces Advanced options to design and logo readiness with no charge when assets already exist.
- Recalibrates website creation prices for a Bulgarian freelancer offer.
- Adds an admin Service Pricing editor for the complete public catalogue.
- Applies saved website and support prices to their corresponding live estimators while retaining competitive defaults when no override exists.
- Renames Website Services to Lab Services.
- Adds a mobile-friendly PC Services coming-soon card for future remote support.
