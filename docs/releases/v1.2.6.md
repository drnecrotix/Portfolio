# v1.2.6

GDPR, privacy and legal transparency for NecrotixLab.

## Legal pages

- Adds `/legal` as the public legal overview.
- Adds `/privacy` with GDPR-focused data-processing information based on the actual application flows.
- Adds `/cookies` documenting first-party cookies, session storage, local storage and third-party embedded media.
- Adds `/terms` with public-site, comment, AI assistant, external-service and intellectual-property terms.
- Adds canonical metadata for all legal routes.
- Adds all four legal routes to the sitemap.

## GDPR transparency

- Documents contact-form processing, optional private comment email, pseudonymous Blog/Gallery Like identifiers, aggregate views and temporary IP rate limits.
- Documents AI assistant processing and the fact that user messages can be sent to the configured OpenAI, Groq, Google Gemini or compatible custom provider.
- Documents relevant retention behavior visible in the current codebase, including the two-year Like cookies and session-scoped chat/view state.
- Explains data-subject rights and the right to complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP / КЗЛД).
- Does not claim that publication of legal pages alone guarantees complete GDPR compliance; operational provider agreements, real retention practices, security and controller identity remain organisational responsibilities.

## Collection points

- Updates the Contact form privacy checkbox to link directly to the Privacy & GDPR Policy and describe processing as transparency rather than relying on generic consent wording.
- Adds Privacy and Terms links directly inside the Blog comment composer.
- Adds a permanent `Legal & Privacy` link to the expanded footer even when older saved footer settings do not contain it.

## Cookies and external media

- Documents the current first-party storage used for locale, Likes, private access, authentication, admin preferences, first-visit state, view markers, chat history and theme preferences.
- Notes that third-party Gallery embeds can connect visitors to YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, Pinterest or Dailymotion and can be subject to those providers' own cookies and privacy rules.
- The release does not add an advertising-cookie banner because the audited code does not intentionally set first-party advertising or cross-site behavioural advertising cookies.
- Consent-gating third-party embedded media remains a recommended follow-up for a stricter ePrivacy posture.

## Release

- Version `1.2.6`.
- Admin updater-ready after merge through `package.json`.
- No Prisma migration.
- No new dependencies.
