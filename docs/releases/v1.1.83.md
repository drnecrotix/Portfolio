# v1.1.83 - Wiki CMS

## Wiki architecture

- Keeps `/wiki` as the permanent main biography article.
- Adds `/wiki/articles` as the searchable Wiki catalog.
- Adds public article routes at `/wiki/<slug>`.
- Supports Wiki categories: Person, Project, Community, Organization, Creative work, FAQ, Technology and Other.
- Uses internal `Page` records prefixed with `__wiki-article-` so the feature needs no Prisma migration.
- Draft, Review, Published and Archived states reuse the existing CMS status system.

## Admin

- Converts `Admin → Wiki` to a compact list-first manager instead of a long settings page.
- Pins the biography as the Main article and lists all additional Wiki articles below it.
- Adds search and category filtering plus a dedicated New article flow.
- Moves main-biography editing to `/admin/wiki/main` with compact collapsible controls.
- Adds rich Tiptap-based editing for biography introduction, biography sections, Wiki article bodies and FAQ answers.
- Rich tools include headings, bold, italic, strike, lists, quotes, inline/code blocks, links, dividers, undo/redo and clear formatting.
- Adds article image, quick-facts infobox, related Wiki articles, featured state and optional SEO overrides.
- FAQ articles support editable question/answer entries.
- Internal Wiki records stay hidden from the generic Pages manager.

## Navigation

- Removes automatic Wiki menu insertion.
- The legacy v1.1.82 auto Wiki navigation signature is suppressed.
- `/wiki` and `/wiki/articles` can be added manually from the existing Navigation manager at any desired position.

## Public design

- Preserves the Wikipedia-inspired visual language of the main biography.
- Reduces the profile portrait to a compact 160–176px presentation instead of a full-width image.
- Adds `Browse Wiki`, Main article and All articles navigation without turning `/wiki` into an index page.
- Additional articles use the same visual system with optional infobox and automatic Heading 2/3 contents navigation.

## Search and SEO

- `/wiki` remains `index, follow` with a canonical URL.
- Adds `Person` and `ProfilePage` JSON-LD to the biography.
- `/wiki/articles` is indexable and canonical.
- Published, indexable Wiki articles receive individual canonical metadata and Article JSON-LD.
- FAQ articles with FAQ entries also emit `FAQPage` structured data.
- Adds `/wiki/articles` and published indexable Wiki articles to the automatic sitemap.
- Generic sitemap/page queries exclude all internal `__*` CMS records.
- Existing `robots.txt` policy continues to allow public Wiki routes while blocking `/admin/` and `/api/` when site indexing is enabled.

## Release

- Version `1.1.83`.
- No Prisma migration.
- No dependency changes.
- Compatible with the existing updater and N0C WASM build path.
