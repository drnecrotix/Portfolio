# Portfolio v1.2.17

## Premium Digital Store

- Redesigns the public Store catalog with a more premium, image-first marketplace layout inspired by modern handmade/digital marketplaces without copying third-party branding.
- Adds Store search, category filters and sorting.
- Makes product cards more compact and visual, with Featured/Free badges, instant-delivery metadata and improved hover states.
- Polishes individual product pages with stronger imagery, purchase panel hierarchy and protected delivery information.

## Product editor

- Keeps product saves in-place through the existing server-action/AJAX flow instead of a browser form navigation.
- Adds visible success/error toast notifications and a sticky save-status bar.
- Adds unsaved-change state and keeps FormDraftGuard recovery.
- Automatically generates a slug from the title until the slug is manually edited.
- Reorganizes the editor into listing, media, delivery, publishing, payment and SEO sections.
- Keeps Media Library cover selection.

## Digital delivery

- Adds two delivery modes: local private upload and Masked external link.
- External links must use HTTPS and cannot point to localhost or obvious private-network addresses.
- The original external source URL is kept server-side inside the Store storage key and is never rendered to Store customers.
- Customer downloads continue through the access-token `/store/download/...` endpoint. External files are fetched and streamed by the Necrotix Lab server, so the original source URL is not exposed in the customer-facing download link.
- Local private storage and legacy R2 fallback remain compatible.

## Cart and digital-content consent

- Adds per-item selection checkboxes and Select all/Clear controls to the cart.
- Requires acceptance of the Terms & Digital Content Policy before checkout or free-download delivery begins.
- Direct Buy/Download actions require the same consent.
- Checkout API rejects requests that do not explicitly include digital-terms consent.

## Terms / refund policy

- Adds a Digital Store, delivery and refunds section to Terms of Use.
- States that NecrotixLab does not offer discretionary refunds for digital products after protected access/download has begun following the customer’s express request for immediate delivery.
- Explicitly preserves mandatory consumer remedies for faulty, unavailable or non-conforming digital content under applicable law.

## Admin navigation

- Fixes the Commerce navigation bug where Digital Store and Orders were both highlighted on `/admin/store/orders`.
- Nested navigation now highlights only the most specific matching item on desktop and mobile.

## Compatibility

- No Prisma migration is required.
- Existing local Store files, legacy R2 files, Creem mappings and Lemon Squeezy mappings remain compatible.
