# v1.2.10

Refines the analytics and administration experience introduced in v1.2.9 by making Dashboard traffic easier to read, simplifying Experiments, and reducing Dashboard clutter.

## Dashboard traffic

- Replaces the Dashboard timeline with an interactive Monday-to-Sunday traffic view.
- Supports 7-day and 30-day ranges for the weekday pattern.
- Shows average page views, average visits, pages per visit and the peak weekday.
- Weekday bars can be hovered, focused or selected to inspect a specific day.

## Dashboard cleanup

- Removes the standalone statistics strip from the top of Dashboard.
- Keeps Projects, Posts, Pages, Media and Draft posts as a compact Content snapshot inside Site Health.
- Removes the standalone AI Assistant card from Dashboard.
- Reduces Public cache to a single `Purge public cache` action inside Site Health.
- Keeps AI Assistant and Revision history accessible through Quick Access.

## Experiments

- Separates A/B tests and Audience analytics into two clear views.
- Simplifies each experiment around its primary conversion, current signal, B vs A lift and sample size.
- Moves sample warnings and supporting events into expandable details.
- Adds overview counters for active tests, total exposures, tests ready to evaluate and split warnings.

## Country analytics

- Makes the country panel interactive: Top countries can be selected and their views, visits and share are shown explicitly.
- Highlights the selected country on the audience globe.
- Makes unattributed country traffic visible instead of silently showing an empty map.
- Broadens supported reverse-proxy and edge country headers.
- Keeps analytics country-level only. City, precise location and raw IP addresses are not stored.

## Release

- Version `1.2.10`.
- No new Prisma migration.
- No new runtime dependencies.
