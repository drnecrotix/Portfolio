# v1.1.91 - Context-aware Wiki header shortcuts

## Wiki header navigation

- Keeps four primary destinations in the Wiki ecosystem: Main article, Articles, FAQ and Career Dossier.
- Hides the destination that represents the page currently open on the three Wiki root pages.
- `/wiki` shows Articles, FAQ and Career Dossier.
- `/wiki/articles` shows Main article, FAQ and Career Dossier.
- `/wiki/faq` shows Main article, Articles and Career Dossier.
- Keeps exactly three actionable shortcuts in the top header on mobile and desktop.
- Leaves the internal Contents/sidebar navigation unchanged so the current Wiki context remains visible where useful.
